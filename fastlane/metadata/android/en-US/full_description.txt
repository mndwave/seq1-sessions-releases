<p>Start with a chat. Grow into a computer.</p>
<p><b>One conversation, everywhere.</b> Your chat follows you across your phone and your Mac, so you can pick up exactly where you left off.</p>
<p><b>A computer when you need one.</b> Ask for more and SEQ1 Sessions works in your own private environment: files you choose, a full terminal (SSH and mosh), and email drafts and your calendar, only as far as you grant access.</p>
<p><b>Sign in your way.</b> Use the link or six-digit code from your invitation email, or approve with your own Nostr key in Amber (NIP-55) or another remote signer (NIP-46). There is no password to look after.</p>
<p><b>Private by design.</b> This build contains no Google libraries: no Google Play services and no Firebase. Notifications arrive over UnifiedPush or the app's own live connection.</p>
<p>SEQ1 Sessions is available by invitation. Ask for yours at sessions.seq1.net.</p>
